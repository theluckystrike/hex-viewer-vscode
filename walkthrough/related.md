# Files that often come with binary files

- Tensor Viewer (.npy, .safetensors or .gguf files): Shows NPY, NPZ, Safetensors and GGUF tensors with stats and heatmaps.
- ZIP Explorer (.zip, .tar or .jar archives): Browses ZIP, JAR, VSIX, TAR and GZ files, opens entries in tabs and extracts them safely.
- PCAP Viewer (.pcap or .pcapng captures): Opens PCAP and PCAPNG captures with a packet list, filters and a hex pane.

Each one is a separate free extension. Nothing installs on its own.
